# Home Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

## What is covered
- Sudden and accidental water damage, including burst pipes during freezing conditions.
- Fire damage, including smoke damage to adjoining rooms, provided a fire department report is filed.
- Storm damage to the roof and structure, including fallen tree branches.
- Theft or attempted break-in, including damage to doors/windows caused during the break-in, provided a police report is filed.
- Personal belongings inside the home, up to 50% of the dwelling coverage limit.

## What is NOT covered
- Gradual water damage from long-term leaks or lack of maintenance (this is treated as a maintenance issue, not a claim).
- Flood damage from external water sources (rivers, storm surge) — requires separate flood insurance.
- Damage from pest infestation (termites, rodents).
- Any loss during a period the home was vacant for more than 60 consecutive days, unless a vacancy rider was purchased.

## Claim requirements
- Police report for break-ins/theft.
- Fire department report for fire-related claims.
- Photos/video of the damage, ideally before any cleanup begins.
- Repair estimate from a licensed contractor.

## Deductibles
- Standard claims: $1,000
- Named-storm/hurricane claims: 2% of dwelling coverage value
