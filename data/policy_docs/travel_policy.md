# Travel Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

## What is covered
- Trip cancellation due to a documented medical emergency, covering non-refundable bookings.
- Flight cancellations/delays caused by weather or airline fault, covering reasonable additional hotel and meal expenses (up to $150/day).
- Lost or delayed luggage, covering essential replacement items, provided a report is filed with the airline within 24 hours.
- Emergency medical treatment required while abroad, including clinic visits and prescribed medication.

## What is NOT covered
- Trip cancellation for reasons not documented as a medical or family emergency (e.g., change of plans).
- Pre-existing medical conditions unless a pre-existing condition waiver was purchased at the time of booking.
- Loss of cash or valuables left unattended in checked luggage.
- Any claim where the traveler continued the trip after a covered cancellation event without notifying the insurer.

## Claim requirements
- Airline's official cancellation/delay notice, or the airline's lost-luggage report reference number.
- Receipts for any additional expenses claimed.
- Medical documentation for emergency treatment or cancellation claims.

## Deductibles
- Standard claims: $50
- Medical emergency claims: no deductible
