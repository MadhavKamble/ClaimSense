# Health Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

## What is covered
- Emergency room visits, including imaging and diagnostics performed during the visit.
- Hospitalization, including room charges, nursing care, and medication administered during the stay.
- Pre-authorized surgeries and the associated post-operative physiotherapy sessions (up to 10 sessions).
- Specialist consultations for chronic condition management, up to 12 visits per policy year.
- Prescribed medication directly related to a covered diagnosis or treatment.

## What is NOT covered
- Elective/cosmetic procedures not medically necessary.
- Any treatment received without prior authorization where authorization was required (e.g., non-emergency surgery).
- Alternative/experimental treatments not approved by the relevant medical board.
- Claims submitted more than 90 days after the date of treatment.

## Claim requirements
- Itemized hospital/clinic bill.
- Physician's diagnosis and treatment notes.
- Pre-authorization reference number, where applicable.
- Original prescription/medication receipts for medication claims.

## Deductibles
- Standard claims: $250 per incident
- Chronic condition management: no deductible after annual $500 threshold is met
