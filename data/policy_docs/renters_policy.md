# Renters Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

## What is covered
- Personal belongings damaged or destroyed by fire, water damage originating from another unit, or a covered storm event.
- Theft or break-in at the rented unit, provided a police report is filed.
- Temporary living expenses if the rented unit becomes uninhabitable due to a covered event.
- Liability coverage if a guest is injured inside the rented unit.

## What is NOT covered
- Damage to the building structure itself (this is the landlord's responsibility, not the tenant's policy).
- Belongings damaged due to the tenant's own negligence (e.g., leaving a window open during a storm).
- Bedbug or pest-related damage.
- Business equipment or inventory kept in the rented unit for commercial use, unless a business-property rider was purchased.

## Claim requirements
- Police report for theft/break-in claims.
- Photos of damaged belongings and, where possible, original purchase receipts.
- Landlord or building management confirmation for building-originated incidents (e.g., upstairs neighbor's leak).

## Deductibles
- Standard claims: $500
