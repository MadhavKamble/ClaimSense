# Auto Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

## What is covered
- Collision damage to the insured vehicle, regardless of fault, up to the policy limit.
- Damage caused by another driver, provided a police report is filed within 48 hours.
- Windshield and glass repair, subject to a separate lower deductible than standard collision claims.
- Theft of the vehicle or components (e.g., stereo systems, catalytic converters), provided a police report is filed.
- Weather-related damage (hail, flooding) under comprehensive coverage add-on only.

## What is NOT covered
- Damage occurring while the vehicle was being used for commercial purposes (ride-sharing, delivery) unless a commercial rider is attached.
- Mechanical breakdown unrelated to an accident (wear and tear, engine failure).
- Damage while driving under the influence — claim will be denied and may affect policy renewal.
- Personal belongings left inside the vehicle during a theft (covered under renters/home policy instead, if applicable).

## Claim requirements
- Police report number for any theft or multi-vehicle collision claim.
- Photos of the damage.
- Repair estimate from an approved body shop, or two independent estimates for claims above $5,000.

## Deductibles
- Standard collision: $500
- Glass-only claims: $100
- Comprehensive (weather/theft): $250
